# London Vision Clinic – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

London Vision Clinic is a specialist private ophthalmology and vision correction clinic based on Harley Street, London. Founded in 2002 by Professor Dan Reinstein, the clinic provides advanced laser eye surgery, refractive procedures and lens-based treatments, including SMILE Pro, LASIK, PRESBYOND and cataract surgery.

The redesigned homepage focuses on communicating the clinic's specialist expertise, simplifying treatment discovery and making the patient decision-making process clearer. It is designed for prospective patients exploring vision correction and specialist eye surgery.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. More Focused Homepage Messaging
- Replaced repetitive promotional messaging with a clearer, more focused introduction.
- Strengthened the presentation of the clinic's specialist positioning and refractive surgery expertise.
- Improved the prominence of consultation and treatment exploration calls to action.

**Problem addressed:** Repetitive messaging previously weakened the impact of the homepage introduction and made its core value proposition less distinctive.

### 2. Improved Treatment Navigation
- Organised the treatment offering into clearer categories.
- Introduced visual treatment cards with concise explanations.
- Made individual procedures easier to identify and explore, including SMILE Pro, LASIK, PRK/LASEK, PRESBYOND and lens-based treatments.

**Problem addressed:** The range of treatments was difficult to navigate, making it harder for visitors to identify relevant procedures.

### 3. Clearer Patient Decision-Making Journey
- Improved the presentation of consultation information, treatment pricing and patient aftercare.
- Structured the patient journey into clearer steps.
- Increased the visibility of contact and consultation calls to action, including mobile accessibility.

**Problem addressed:** Important decision-making information was distributed across the website, making it harder for prospective patients to understand the process and take the next step.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Created a more refined and professional appearance suited to a specialist Harley Street eye surgery clinic.
- **Homepage Layout:** Improved content hierarchy and section organisation around the clinic's expertise, treatments and patient journey.
- **Treatment Presentation:** Used structured treatment categories and visual cards to make complex service information easier to scan.
- **User Experience:** Improved navigation between treatment information, consultation details and contact options.
- **Content Consistency:** Established a clearer messaging structure and more consistent presentation of the clinic's services.
- **Overall Appearance:** Shifted the homepage towards a more focused, informative and patient-oriented experience.

---

**Project Focus:** Clearer specialist positioning, easier treatment discovery and a more structured patient decision-making journey.
